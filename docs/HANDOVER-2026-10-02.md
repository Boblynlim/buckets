# Handover: Buckets, 2 October 2026

Written from a Claude Code session in the curriculum project, so work can continue here. Read this first, then `docs/email-import/README.md` and `ROLLOVER_SYSTEM.md`.

## Why this work, in Jaz's words

- Wants spending on herself (facials, skincare, treats) to feel OK: "a plan that helps me feel like this money is ok to be spent".
- "I always say I have no money but I actually do; I'm just not willing to spend on some stuff because it hurts me when I spend, like I don't have enough."
- Has built trackers before but doesn't keep up with them. Buckets expenses stop in July 2026; a happiness journal (another project) stopped in March: "nothing to write, no point, wasn't feeling anything".
- Wants net worth tracked and compared month to month, with goals to work towards: "I think that's lacking."

This is part of a wider personal-growth practice (private notes live outside any repo, in `~/src/tries/personal-growth/`). Don't copy personal details into this repo.

## Design principles that came out of the conversation

1. **No daily input.** Every tracker that needed daily typing faded. Automate first; ask only when the app can't know.
2. **The app does the remembering.** Jaz values things happening without having to prompt them.
3. **Decide once, not per purchase.** Spending guilt ("pain of paying"; Rick, Cryder & Loewenstein 2008) is eased by pre-committed money: a labelled pot, funded on payday, is already approved (mental accounting).
4. **Give something back.** Inputs must feed a visible result (monthly sheet, net worth trend, goal progress), or they stop.
5. **No streaks, no guilt.** Missing a review costs nothing.

## What to build, in priority order

### 1. Learned merchant rules (auto-categorise)
Jaz's rule: auto-file **only if the merchant is repeated and she has filed it before**.
- A merchant qualifies when it has at least 2 confirmed expenses, all in the same bucket with the same `isNecessary` / `worthIt` / `needsVsWants` answers. Inconsistent history → stays in the review queue.
- New pending transactions from a qualifying merchant become expenses automatically, copying bucket and ratings ("auto-log the necessity rating or sth").
- Show auto-filed items in a short "filed for you" list with one-tap undo or move; a move updates the rule.
- One-off backfill: run the rules over the current pending queue (should clear BUS/MRT at least).
- Merchant names vary (masked names like "LIX ZHANGXXXXX", "MX LIX SOXX MEXX" look like PayNow transfers). Normalise conservatively; when unsure, leave it for review.

### 2. Output in Jaz's spreadsheet format
She wants entries to "auto fill in this format". Her sheet has three parts:

**Transactions** (one row each):
`Date | Item | Income | Expenses | Tag | Comments`
- Item is a short human label ("bus/mrt", "yakiniku like", "Facial package", "xh bday").
- Tag is the category. Tags seen: Food, Grocery, Transport, Parents, Enrichment, Entertainment, Gifts, Solo date budget, Shopping, Self care, Insurance, Investment, Pocket Monies, Savings, Shared Acc, Tax, Income.

**Monthly budget summary** (one row per category):
`Category | Roll over sum | Budget | Actual | Difference`
- Rule written in the sheet: "For every category unspent, can roll over up to $20 to next month." Check this against `ROLLOVER_SYSTEM.md`, which may roll over the full amount.
- Plus a per-tag pivot: `Tag | SUM of Expenses | SUM of Income`.

**Net worth** (one row per account or holding):
`Place | Amount In | Amount Now | P/L | Date of Return | Tag | Comments`
- Tag is Liquid, Current or Permanent; pivot by tag with Grand Total.
- Places include bank accounts, fixed deposits, robo-advisors, stocks, T-bills, CPF, insurance policies and property. Real balances are in her sheet; keep them out of the repo.

**Decided (2 Oct):** show these three views **inside the app**, not as an export.

### 3. Net worth over time, and goals
- Monthly snapshot of each account's Amount Now, with month-on-month change by tag (Liquid / Current / Permanent) and in total.
- Low input: a once-a-month prompt to update balances, prefilled with last month's numbers so only changes are typed. Bank alerts that carry balances could fill some automatically later.
- Goals with a target and date (e.g. home renovation fund, emergency fund), showing progress from the snapshots. There are already `save`-mode buckets with `targetAmount`; check whether goals belong there.

### 4. Care bucket and reassurance
- **Decided (2 Oct):** the care pot is the existing **"maintenance"** bucket (spend mode, planned 200/month in prod). Facials, skincare and similar go there. A separate "Self care" bucket (250) also exists; ask whether it should merge into maintenance or stay separate.
- **Packages are lumpy, not monthly** (e.g. a facial package paid up front). That's why maintenance rolls over: unspent months build up the pot so a package is already paid for when it comes. Maintenance must keep full rollover (current app behaviour), not the sheet's $20 cap.
- When an auto-filed purchase lands in it, send a push notification: "Covered by your care pot, $X left. Enjoy it." $X is the whole pot (carryover + this month), not just this month's $200.
- If a package costs more than the pot, don't frame it as overspending. Show it as paid ahead, e.g. "Covered by the next N months of your care pot". The rollover already carries the shortfall forward.
