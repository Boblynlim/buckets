## 1. What this is

A personal money app for one person. The purpose, in one line:

> Live my lifestyle without feeling the pinch, while my net worth grows.

It started as "Buckets", a general budgeting app with chat, insights, reports and daily prompts. In early October it was reshaped into something smaller and calmer: pots of money shown as pottery cups on shelves, filled on payday and spent without guilt.

### Why it exists
- All money sits in one main bank account, so the app is the only thing keeping savings and spending money apart.
- The owner tends to feel she "has no money" when she does, and finds spending on herself painful. Money that's set aside in advance (a labelled pot, filled on payday) feels already approved, so spending it doesn't hurt. Research: "pain of paying" (Rick, Cryder & Loewenstein 2008) and mental accounting.
- Every earlier tracker that needed daily typing was abandoned.

### Design principles (agreed, apply to every feature)
1. **No daily input.** Automate first; ask only when the app can't know.
2. **The app does the remembering.**
3. **Decide once, not per purchase.** Pots are funded on payday; spending from them needs no thought.
4. **Every input gives something back** (a filled cup, a net worth line, goal progress), or it stops being used.
5. **No streaks, no guilt.** Missing a check-in costs nothing. Overspending is framed as "paid ahead", not failure.

## 2. Product decisions

### The app is four things
1. **Home**
2. **Cup sheet** (tap a cup)
3. **Payday check-in**
4. **Settings**

Plus a small **+** button to add a spend, and the net worth sheet off Home.

**Cut (decided 3 Oct):** Shrimpy chat, Insights, Reports, growth letters, daily prompts, "worth it" / necessity ratings, the Add tab. The review queue now lives inside the check-in. Old code for these still exists (see section 5).

### Home, top to bottom
- Month pill (tap for a sliding strip of past months; past months show what was spent).
- Big number: **"yours to spend this month"** = what's left in the Everyday and For me shelves. Not total cash.
- "N to file" row when the importer needs help; "money in to sort" row for deposits.
- **Saved:** emergency fund, reno goal (+ the reno backup kept in the bank), next adventure.
- **Net worth** line with change since last month → opens the net worth sheet.
- **Three shelves of cups:** Everyday, For me, Saving up.
- **Fixed** and **Investing**: money that leaves before spending. Shown as a checklist, not cups (see section 3).
- Maker's mark (easter egg).

### Cups
- Every cup carries unspent money forward **in full** (no cap). The old spreadsheet rule of "roll over up to $20" was rejected.
- Shelves group cups by how the spending feels:
  - **Everyday:** just runs out.
  - **For me:** guilt-free, "already paid for, enjoy it".
  - **Saving up:** fills slowly for lumpy costs ("is it ready yet?").
- Travel is split into **Family trips** (obligations) and **Adventures** (her own). Adventures is an ordered list: each item has a cost and a target date, and items are funded in order.
- A purchase bigger than the pot is shown as covered by future months, not as overspending.
- Bills and regular investing are **not** cups. They live in the Fixed and Investing groups.

### Goals
- Emergency fund (kept in instant-access accounts).
- Reno: a cash goal with a deadline. Kept separate from net worth. Fixed deposits are never touched for it. Extra income above the usual pay goes straight to Reno.
- Adventures (above).
- The long-term net worth target was reset to something realistic.

### Net worth
- Accounts are grouped by **when the money can be used**: **Now** (bank accounts), **Soon** (fixed deposits), **Later** (investments, retirement, property).
- One balance per account per month. Monthly update is prefilled from last month, so only the accounts that changed need typing.
- Investments show what was **put in** vs what it's **worth now**.
- History was seeded from the old spreadsheet and checked against bank statements (6 Oct).

### Importing spending (no daily input)
- Bank alert emails are forwarded to `POST /import-email` (Convex http) via Apps Script (`docs/email-import/`).
- **Learned filing:** a merchant files itself after being filed **once**. Payment-processor names (Qashier, Pine Payment, NETS…) always ask, because one name covers many shops.
- Auto-filed spends say "filed for you" in the cup sheet; tapping lets you move them, and a move updates the rule.
- **Money in:** deposits of $3,000 or more count as income. Smaller ones ask: **Paid back** (reduces a spend), **A refund** (not income; remembers the sender), or **Income**. Also "Split it" and "Can't remember".
- Card bill payments and transfers between her own accounts are not spending.
- **Payday:** salary arrives as a transfer from Wise. That email sends a push notification that opens the check-in. Push needs Notifications on in Settings, and on iPhone the app must be added to the home screen.

### Payday check-in flow
Recap of last month (cups drain to what was left, "$X stays in your cups") → balances (prefilled) → "filed for you" review → the few it couldn't file (tap a cup, card drops in) → numbers count up, cups fill.

## 3. Design system

The rules live in `src/screens/home/homeStyles.ts`. Everything new should use them.

- **Look:** sleek, clean, uncluttered, with a few physical touches (pottery cups, walnut shelves, a wooden cubby keypad, cup clinks, a clay-throwing loader). Past attempts were called "plain", "ugly" and "cluttered". Show screenshots, not descriptions of options.
- **Ground:** flat warm off-white wall `#F3F0EA`, no texture. White cards for grouped rows (Settings style). Pop-up sheets use the wall colour. The date picker stays white because it opens on top of another sheet.
- **One font:** Schibsted Grotesk, weights 400/500/600. Weight and size carry hierarchy.
- **Colours: use `COLORS`, never raw hex.**

| Token | Use |
|---|---|
| `wall` | page and sheet background |
| `ink` | espresso #2B1D15: text, numbers, the logo (was near-black #1F1B17 until 8 Oct) |
| `espresso` | the same espresso, for dark fills: buttons, selected chips, toggles, the dock |
| `muted` | labels, secondary text |
| `faint` | placeholders, empty values ("–"), version text |
| `hairline` | dividers between rows |
| `line` | outlines: chips, input underlines, empty rings |
| `sand` | soft fill for icon buttons and quiet pills |
| `sheet` | white cards and rows |
| `green` | good / done / paid |
| `rust` | low, danger, negative change |
| `scrim` | dim backdrop behind sheets |

  The net worth sheet has its own warmer chart colours (tuned 6 Oct). Left alone on purpose.
- **Section headers:** 13px `muted` label on the left, 13px summary on the right (e.g. "$449 left", "$0 of $2,431 paid"). Shelves, Fixed and Investing all follow this.
- **Motion** (from the "bobland" rules): deceleration easing only (`cubic-bezier(0.16,0.9,0.4,1)`), no bounce, blur-fades. Everything stops under reduced-motion.
- **Layout safety:** nothing inside a scrolling column or a sheet may shrink (`.bk-scroll > *, .bk-sheet > * { flex-shrink: 0 }`). A child that *should* scroll inside a sheet needs inline `flex: '1 1 auto', minHeight: 0`.
- **Sheets:** close on backdrop tap and on Escape (`useEscape(onClose)` in homeStyles).
- **Numbers:** whole dollars for balances and totals (`money`), cents for single transactions (`moneyExact`). Avoid `tabular-nums` on short amounts; Schibsted's tabular comma looks gappy ("$1 , 100").
- **Bottom bar:** the espresso dock (section 4). Custom-drawn icons only, never stock-style ones. Small delights over decoration: the + turning to ×, and a glowing clay dot on the + when there's something to file.
- **Phone:** web app on iPhone. Respect `SAFE_TOP` (Dynamic Island) and the bottom safe area. Inputs are 16px+ so iOS doesn't zoom.

### Easter eggs (keep them quiet and pottery-themed)
- Login: each passcode digit is a cup with its own clink (pentatonic, so any code sounds nice).
- Home, very bottom: the Moru wordmark, small and faint, like a signature on the base of a pot. Tap it and it redraws with a clink; after three taps it says "Made one cup at a time."
- Settings: tap "Made with care by Jaz" and a cup is thrown on the wheel ("Every cup starts as a lump of clay.").

## 8. Renaming

Why change: "Buckets" sounds like a generic budgeting tool. What's been built is calmer and more personal: handmade cups on a shelf, filled on payday, spent without guilt.

Shortlist (8 Oct):

| Name | Why |
|---|---|
| **Cupboard** (recommended) | Literally cups on boards: the walnut shelves. It's also where you keep what you need. Warm and domestic, and it matches the visuals. |
| **Enough** | Names the feeling the app exists for: "I have enough, and this is OK to spend." Strong, but more abstract. |
| **Pour** | The payday moment: money poured into each cup. A short, gentle verb. |
| **Kiln** | Where clay becomes a cup: decisions set once, then they hold. More craft than calm. |

**Decided 8 Oct: Moru.** Written in English letters. It comes from a Japanese word meaning to protect, look after, or cherish things, keeping and caring for them instead of throwing them away. That's the app's job: looking after money so it can be spent without worry, the same way you'd look after a handmade cup.

Renamed where it shows: `public/index.html` (title, apple-mobile-web-app-title, description), `public/manifest.json`, `app.json`, the login wordmark in `App.web.tsx`, the Settings footer, the Home maker's mark, push notification titles in `convex/pushNotificationActions.ts`, and README/docs. Done: tab title and description, home-screen name (manifest, apple title), login wordmark, Settings footer, Home maker's mark, the service worker's default push title, `app.json` displayName. Left on purpose: `app.json` `name` (the native module name), code identifiers (`buckets` table, `Bucket` types), the repo, and the Convex and Vercel project names.
